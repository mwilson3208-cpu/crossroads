What your sponsorship pays for

YOLI Leadership Academy matches Valley teenagers with trained mentors, puts them in charge of a real community service project, and sends every graduate out with a written plan for college and career. Families pay nothing. Sponsors and donors fund the seats: mentor training, scholarships, meeting spaces and supplies, and hands-on leadership and service opportunities.
