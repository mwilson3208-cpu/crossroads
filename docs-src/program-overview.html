How it works

  1. Apply. A short form. A real person calls within three days.
  2. Match with a mentor. A trained adult whose path fits the student's goals.
  3. Lead a project. Plan and run a community service project, then present it in public.
  4. Graduate with a plan. One page: the path, the deadlines, the money, and the people who will help.

What students do each month

  • Weekly contact with their mentor, focused on the student's own goals.
  • A leadership session with the full class. [CONFIRM cadence and location]
  • Work on the service project, from picking the problem to delivering the result.
  • Practice speaking in front of a group.
  • One concrete step on the written plan: a campus visit, a financial aid deadline, or a conversation with someone in the field.
