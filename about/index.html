How Crossroads started.

Crossroads Leadership Academy started with a simple belief. The Rio Grande Valley is full of teenagers who can lead, and too few of them have an adult outside the family who has already walked the road they want to take.

Co-founder Cristina Solis Wilson grew up in the Valley and spent a career leading soldiers, where leadership is taught, practiced, and earned. Crossroads was built so Valley teenagers can learn it from Valley adults, at no cost to their families.

The flagship is YOLI Leadership Academy. Students are matched with a mentor, lead a real service project, and leave with a written plan for college, a trade, the military, or a business of their own.

In 2026 we relaunched after a rebuild. The goal has not changed: every teenager in the Valley gets someone in their corner.
