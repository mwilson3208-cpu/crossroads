How it works.

Four steps, from the first form to the last day.

  1. Apply

    Fill out a short form. A real person calls within three days to talk with the student and the family.

  2. Match with a mentor

    Each student is paired with a trained adult whose path lines up with the student's goals.

  3. Lead a project

    Students plan and run a community service project, then present the results in public.

  4. Graduate with a plan

    Every graduate leaves with a one-page plan: the path, the deadlines, the money, and the people who will help.

Questions parents ask.

How much does it cost my family?

Nothing. Scholarships cover participation for every student, and local donors and sponsors fund those scholarships. There are no fees and no supplies to buy.

Does my child need good grades to get in?

No. Students do not need perfect grades. We look for students who are willing to show up and do the work. [CONFIRM eligibility requirements]

Who are the mentors?

Adults from the Valley who have finished school, built careers, served in the military, or run businesses. Every mentor is trained before being matched with a student.
