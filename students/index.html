For students

This is for you if you are tired of waiting for permission.

You do not need perfect grades. You do not need a plan yet. You need to show up, do the work, and let someone who has been where you want to go help you get there.

Apply Ask a Question

What you will actually do.

  • Meet with your mentor every week and work on goals you pick.
  • Plan and run a service project in your community, with a team you lead.
  • Stand up and present your project in front of a real audience.

What it costs you.

Nothing but your time and your word.

No fees. Nothing to buy. Scholarships cover all of it. What we ask is simple: show up, do the work, and keep your word.

What you get.

  • A mentor who knows your name and your goals.
  • A project you led, with results you can point to.
  • A written plan for what comes after high school.
  • People who will write your recommendation letters.

Apply.

Two minutes. Then a real person calls you within three days. We will talk with your parent or guardian too.
