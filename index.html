What stands between a Valley teenager and a future they chose.

01

No one to call.

Most of our students have never had an adult outside their family ask what they want to do with their life, then help them get there.

02

No map.

College applications, financial aid, trades, and military service all have deadlines and paperwork nobody explained. Missing one can close the door.

03

No money for the extras.

Leadership camps, test prep, and campus visits cost money. Families choosing between those and rent choose rent. We make sure they don't have to choose.

Why we built this.

I grew up in the Rio Grande Valley. I know what it costs a young person to have no one in their corner who has already been where they want to go. I spent my career leading soldiers, and I learned that leadership is taught, practiced, and earned. It is not reserved for people with the right last name or the right zip code. Crossroads exists so that every teenager in the Valley has someone who will teach it to them, hold them to it, and open the first door. That is the whole mission.

Cristina Solis WilsonCo-Founder and Executive Director
